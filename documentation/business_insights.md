# Business Insights & Recommendations

## Key Findings

- **7,043 customers** were analyzed, with an overall churn rate of **26.54%**.
- Churn is highest among **month-to-month customers** and customers with **0–12 months of tenure**.
- **Electronic check** and **fiber optic** customers show elevated churn.
- Churned customers have a higher average monthly charge (**$74.44**) than retained customers (**$61.27**).
- The combination of **month-to-month + fiber optic + electronic check + 0–12 months tenure** shows particularly high churn (**71.16%**).
- A more concentrated segment also identified customers lacking **Online Security and Tech Support**, with churn reaching approximately **74.45%**.

## Recommendations

1. Prioritize retention efforts during the **first 12 months**.
2. Encourage month-to-month customers to move to **one- or two-year contracts**.
3. Investigate pricing, service experience, and onboarding for high-risk fiber-optic customers.
4. Review the customer experience associated with **electronic check** payments.
5. Consider targeted offers for **Online Security and Tech Support** where appropriate.

## Business Focus

The strongest opportunity is **proactive retention of new, month-to-month customers**, particularly those using fiber optic and electronic check.

## Caveat

These findings show **associations, not causation**. Further analysis would be required to determine why these factors are linked to churn.
