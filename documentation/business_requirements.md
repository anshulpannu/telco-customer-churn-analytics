Business objective

Understand customer churn patterns and identify customer/service characteristics associated with higher churn, with the ultimate goal of informing retention strategies.

Key questions:

What is the overall customer churn rate?
Which customer segments have the highest churn?
How does churn vary by contract type?
Which services are associated with higher/lower churn?
How does tenure relate to churn?
What revenue/customer value is at risk?
Why are customers leaving?
