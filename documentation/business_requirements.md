# Business Requirements

## Business Objective

Analyze customer churn for a fictional telecommunications company to identify customer, service, contract, and billing characteristics associated with higher churn and highlight potential retention opportunities.

The analysis will use SQL as the primary analytical tool, with Power BI used to present the key findings.

## Business Questions

The analysis will focus on:

1. What is the overall customer churn rate?
2. Which customer segments have higher or lower churn rates?
3. How does churn vary by tenure, contract type, and Internet service?
4. Are monthly charges and other service characteristics associated with higher churn?
5. What are the main reasons customers give for leaving?
6. Which customer segments represent the greatest potential customer value at risk?

## Key Metrics

The main metrics will include:

* Total customers
* Churned customers
* Churn rate
* Retained customers
* Average tenure
* Average monthly charges
* Total monthly charges from churned customers
* Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV) of churned customers

Additional metrics may be developed if they provide useful business insight.

## Scope

The analysis will cover customer demographics, tenure, subscribed services, contract type, billing, charges, churn status, churn reasons, and customer value.

The project will focus on identifying patterns and associations in the available data. Findings should not be interpreted as proof that a particular factor directly causes customer churn.

## Dataset Limitation

The dataset contains customer information for a single quarter (Q3). It therefore supports customer-level and segment-level churn analysis but does not provide sufficient historical information for analyzing churn trends over multiple periods.

## Expected Output

The project will produce:

* A cleaned and documented analytical dataset
* SQL-based analysis and customer segmentation
* Retention and customer-value insights
* A Power BI dashboard summarizing the key findings
* Business recommendations based on the analysis
